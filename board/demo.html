stepchess / board

Dependency-free board elements: drag with a mouse or a finger, tap then tap, arrows with the right button or a long press, keyboard and screen reader.

Playmode="play"

You play White; the demo opponent grabs the biggest capture or moves at random. The bar shows chances by material.

Puzzlemode="puzzle"

White to play and mate in two. The board plays the opponent's reply itself.

Analysismode="analysis"

Both sides move. The record with a variation winds the board; the explanation draws arrows step by step.

Diagrammode="view"

Display only: every kind of arrow, marked squares and move glyphs, seen from Black's side.

Set upmode="setup"

Pick a piece in the palette and tap squares to place it; drag a piece on the board, or off it to remove it.

Piecespieces.js

An original set: twelve SVGs colored by CSS custom properties.
